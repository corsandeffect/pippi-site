# PIPPI: Sensory Screen Tint & Contrast Suppressor

A health-first accessibility tool that applies a sensory shield with ambient screen tints, contrast suppression and layout motion freeze for help with eye strain and migraines. Transform your Chrome browser into a gentle, non-intrusive workspace, allowing you to surf and work more comfortably. 

---

## 🛡️ Key Features
* **Out-of-the-Box Relief:** Get immediate sensory relief across a predefined list of over 50 of your favorite platforms, grouped logically for instant protection (for the full list of automatically supported sites, please visit our website [GitHub.io]):

     — **Search Engines**: google.com, bing.com, duckduckgo.com, baidu.com, yahoo.com;

     — *News, Journalism & Media*: bbc.com / bbc.co.uk, nytimes.com, theguardian.com, cnn.com, reuters.com, huffpost.com, washingtonpost.com, time.com,                                               theatlantic.com, economist.com;

     — *Business & Finance*: forbes.com, bloomberg.com, wsj.com;

     — *AI Platforms*: chatgpt.com, claude.ai, openai.com;

     — *Reference, Research & Academia*: wikipedia.org, britannica.com, sciencedirect.com, ncbi.nlm.nih.gov, nih.gov, arxiv.org, archive.org;
  
     — *Software Development & Tech Ecosystems*: github.com, stackoverflow.com, w3schools.com, microsoft.com, mozilla.org, android.com;

     — *Social Media & Online Communities*: reddit.com, linkedin.com, quora.com, tumblr.com, pinterest.com;


* **Sensory Profiles:** Toggle seamlessly between specialized modes. Choose Day Focus for soft-light filtering, Sensory Guard for medium protection, or Deep Calm for advanced multi-layer dimming and layout motion freeze.

* **Layout Motion Freeze (Deep Calm):** Instantly forces CSS animations, smooth-scrolling behaviors, and transition flashes to zero duration — an absolute necessity for mitigating vestibular nausea and sensory strain.

* **Manual Calibration Sliders:** Tailor your environment in real-time with precise slider controls for Tint Warmth and background-dyeing Contrast Suppression.

* **Selective Pause:** Temporarily pause PIPPI on a specific predefined site without needing to toggle off the entire extension.

* **My Custom Sanctuary (20 Slots):** Extend PIPPI's protection beyond our predefined list. Dynamically save and lock your custom calibration settings onto up to 20 additional websites (like personal blogs or local reading domains).

---

## 💡 How To Get Started
1. Click 'Add to Chrome' and pin **PIPPI** to your toolbar.

2. Open **PIPPI** and select your preferred **Sensory Profile** (Day Focus, Sensory Guard, or Deep Calm).

3. If a website is on our predefined list, **PIPPI** applies automatically.

4. To protect a site outside the list, simply click **'Save to Sanctuary'**.

5. Keep surfing!

***Additional Permissions:** When saving a site to My Custom Sanctuary, Chrome will ask you to allow additional permissions. This is strictly required so PIPPI can apply its protective styles to that specific domain. We **do not** read, track, transmit, collect or store any of your personal data or browsing activity whatsoever.

## 🔒 Privacy Policy
Last Updated: October 28, 2026

PIPPI ("we," "our," or "the extension") is built with a strict commitment to user privacy. We can confirm that PIPPI **does not** read, track, transmit, collect, store or monetize any personal data, web content, user activity or browsing activity whatsoever.

### 1. Data Handling
To provide its core functionality, PIPPI interacts with two specific types of data: **User Activity** and **Website Content**. However, PIPPI operates strictly and entirely within your local browser environment which ensures that your digital footprint remains entirely your own.

* **No Browsing Activity:** While the extension utilizes browser APIs to identify active website hostnames, it **does not** record, log or store any activity or a history of the web pages you visit. Hostname evaluation happens strictly in temporary browser memory to determine if a visual shield should be applied.

* **No Telemetry or Analytics:** We **do not** use any third-party tracking scripts, network monitoring, or analytics tools. We have zero visibility into how or when you use the extension.

* **No PII Tracking**: The extension **does not** read, track, transmit, collect or store any of your personal data whatsoever.
  
### 2. Local Processing & Storage
We believe in absolute data minimization. **We do not run a central database or external developer server.** 

* **Local Storage Limits:** To deliver a seamless accessibility workflow across browser sessions, PIPPI saves your functional app preferences locally using the chrome.storage.local API. This data includes:
  
     — Your active sensory profiles (e.g., Day Focus, Sensory Guard, Deep Calm);

     — Granular slider values for your custom tint warmth and contrast suppression;

     — Your 20-slot "My Custom Sanctuary" personal website exception list.

* **Zero Server Footprint:** This configuration data is restricted entirely to your local device. It is **never** uploaded to a cloud database, shared over a network, or made accessible to external entities. You can wipe this data at any time by clearing your extension cache or uninstalling the extension.

### 3. Permissions Disclosures
PIPPI requests specific technical permissions within the browser architecture solely to deliver accessibility functionality:

* **Predefined & Custom Sites:** PIPPI injects local styling into web pages strictly to alter visual layouts, apply color-filtering layers, and layout motion freeze. This is achieved 100% locally on your own device.

* **Storage Permission:** Your preferred sensory profiles and your custom "My Custom Sanctuary" website lists are stored 100% locally on your own device using Chrome’s local storage API.

* **Additional Permissions:** When saving a site to My Custom Sanctuary, Chrome will ask you to allow additional permissions. This is strictly required so PIPPI can apply its protective styles to that specific domain. We **do not** read, track, transmit, collect or store any of your browsing activity whatsoever.
